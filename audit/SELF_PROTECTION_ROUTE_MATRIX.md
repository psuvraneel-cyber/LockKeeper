# Self-Protection & Anti-Tamper Route Matrix

| Route | Detected | Password Gate | Confidence | Android Version Risk | OEM Risk | Policy Risk | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Settings App Info Uninstall** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (Standard across AOSP 10-16) | Low (Button IDs / semantics well-mapped) | High (Play Store A11y policy restricts uninstall prevention) | Intercepts navigation to LockKeeper's app info screen or clicking Uninstall. Requires Admin Password. |
| **2. Settings Apps Force Stop** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (AOSP buttons `button2_negative` / `force_stop`) | Low (OEMs maintain standard app details layouts) | Medium (Service interruption deterrent) | Intercepts clicking Force Stop or App Info details when active. |
| **3. Settings Storage Clear Data / Storage** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (`StorageUseActivity`, `clear_data_button`) | Low (Clear Storage/Data IDs stable) | Low (Preventing app data destruction) | Intercepts entering Storage & Cache / clicking "Clear storage" / "Borrar datos". |
| **4. Settings Disable App** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (`button1_negative`, `disable`) | Low (Disable button semantics shared) | Low (System level app disabling) | Intercepts attempt to disable the application package. |
| **5. Settings Accessibility Disable** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (`ToggleAccessibilityServicePreferenceFragment`) | Medium (MIUI / HyperOS custom preference pages) | Low (Self-preservation of configured accessibility) | Specifically targets LockKeeper's individual toggle page. Explicitly permits general list navigation. |
| **6. Settings Device Admin Deactivation** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (`DeviceAdminAdd`, deactivation flow) | Low (Standard framework component) | Low (Deterrence against unprivileged privilege stripping) | Allowed during onboarding (`isAdminActive == false`). Intercepted when admin is active. |
| **7. Package Installer Uninstall (System Dialog)** | YES | YES | HIGH | Medium (Package installer moved to Google Apex / Mainline) | Medium (OEM package installer replacements) | High (Direct uninstall attempt interception) | Detects `packageinstaller` package with confirmation dialog targeting LockKeeper. Prompts admin overlay or returns home. |
| **8. OEM Package Installer (MIUI, ColorOS, etc.)** | YES | YES | MEDIUM | Medium (Varies across vendor builds) | High (Vendor-custom package manager packages) | High (Direct uninstall interception) | Multi-vendor package matching (`com.miui.securitycenter`, `com.miui.packageinstaller`). Fallback to Home if UI uncontainable. |
| **9. Launcher Drag-to-Uninstall / Direct Uninstall** | PARTIAL | BEST-EFFORT | MEDIUM | High (Launches transient uninstall dialogs via system UI) | High (100s of third-party and vendor launchers) | High (Interfering with standard launcher UX) | When system dialog opens from launcher drag, Package Installer route detects and prompts before confirmation. |
| **10. Launcher Context Menu App Info** | YES | YES | HIGH | Low (Directs to standard Settings App Info) | Low (Redirects to standard Settings) | High (Covered under Route 1) | Clicking "App Info" from launcher enters Route 1 (App Info screen), immediately triggering LockKeeper Gate. |
| **11. Google Play Store Uninstall** | PARTIAL | NO (Passive / Best-effort) | LOW | High (Play Store dynamic webviews and compose layouts) | Low (Google Play UI unified across devices) | EXTREME (Immediate Play Store suspension risk for intercepting Play Store UI) | Policy prohibited from blocking Google Play Store app management. Can detect but deliberately does NOT lock user out of Play Store. |
| **12. Notification / Quick Settings Uninstall/Disable** | PARTIAL | YES | MEDIUM | Medium (Android 13+ foreground services task manager) | Medium (OEM notification shade modifications) | Medium (FGS stop button interception) | Intercepts App Info redirects from notification long-press. Foreground task manager stop requires accessibility node inspection. |
| **13. ADB Shell `pm uninstall` / `pm clear`** | NO | NO | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A | OS-level technical boundary. Non-Device Owner apps cannot intercept USB debugging or root commands. |
| **14. Safe Mode Boot & Uninstall** | NO | NO | N/A | N/A | N/A | N/A | Platform constraint. Third-party accessibility services and overlays are disabled in Safe Mode. |
| **15. Settings App Reset / Permissions Reset** | PARTIAL | BEST-EFFORT | MEDIUM | Low (`ResetApps`, `ResetNetwork`) | Medium (OEM reset menus) | Low (Settings navigation) | Can detect "Reset app preferences" dialog targeting all packages. |
| **16. Dual App / Work Profile Tamper** | PARTIAL | BEST-EFFORT | MEDIUM | High (Managed profiles enforce isolation) | High (Knox / Island / MIUI Second Space) | Low | Interception scoped to current user profile. Cannot monitor cross-profile enterprise containers without Device Owner. |
